About the Network

International context, conceptual foundations, and the strategic role of Latin America in the ethical governance of artificial intelligence.

International Background

UNESCO adopted in 2021 the first Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence, establishing universal principles for the development and use of AI.

The OECD has promoted principles of trustworthy AI, while the Ibero-American Charter on AI adapts these frameworks to the Latin American regional context.

UNESCO

Recomendación sobre Ética de la IA (2021) - Marco universal para una IA centrada en las personas.

OCDE

Principios de IA Confiable - Directrices para sistemas de IA responsables y transparentes.

Carta Iberoamericana de IA

Adaptación regional de principios éticos para el contexto iberoamericano.

El Rol de América Latina

Linkage with Public Policies

CONVERGE-IA maintains an active dialogue with national AI governance initiatives, such as the National Artificial Intelligence Strategy of Costa Rica (ENIA-CR), contributing technical and academic inputs.

Esta articulación permite que los avances académicos informen las políticas públicas, mientras que las necesidades gubernamentales orientan la investigación aplicada de la red.

Institutional Support

AI Governance

Comprehensive support for your institution to develop and implement ethical AI governance frameworks.

Why structured governance?

Without clear governance frameworks, institutions face risks of malpractice, regulatory non-compliance, and loss of trust. CONVERGE-IA offers specialized support to build governance systems adapted to your context.

  • Reduces ethical-legal risks
  • Strengthens internal and external trust
  • Aligns with emerging regulations

Our Support Process

Four structured phases to implement ethical AI governance in your institution

1

Diagnosis

Comprehensive assessment of institutional capabilities, existing policies, and specific AI needs.

~2-3 weeks

2

Participatory Design

Co-creation of the governance framework with internal stakeholders (academics, managers, researchers).

~4-6 weeks

3

Implementation

Launch of the framework, training of key teams, and establishment of processes.

~3-4 months

4

Continuous Monitoring

Tracking, impact assessment, and continuous improvement of the implemented framework.

Sustained
