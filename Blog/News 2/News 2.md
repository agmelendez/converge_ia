## Content Summary

UNED offers a comprehensive self-paced introductory course on the fundamentals, core principles, and practical applications of Artificial Intelligence in academic and educational environments. 

The course is completely free for everyone, providing accessible lifelong learning opportunities for students, educators, and the general community. Participants who successfully complete the coursework and achieve a grade of 8.0 or higher will be awarded an official certificate of completion.

### Key Highlights:
- **Modality:** 100% Virtual / Self-Paced
- **Certification:** Official certificate upon achieving a grade ≥ 8.0
- **Cost:** Free of charge
- **Target Audience:** Professors, researchers, university students, and professionals interested in the ethical foundations of artificial intelligence.
