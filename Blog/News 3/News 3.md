## Content Summary

The University of Costa Rica recently hosted the second "EthicalAI" transnational in-person training. Delivered by international experts from the University of Valencia (Spain) and Sapienza University of Rome (Italy), the event gathered professors, researchers, and university administrators from Costa Rica and Peru to test, validate, and prepare for institutional replication of the project's online Toolkit.

The intensive program followed a structured three-day agenda:

- **Day 1: Introduction & Practice** – Participants were introduced to the AI ethics guides and recommendations, followed by peer-to-peer practical workshops and group debates on applying these principles in real academic scenarios.
- **Day 2: Feedback & Evaluation** – Attendees engaged in collective feedback sessions to identify areas for improvement, assessing the usability and relevance of the guides to make necessary pedagogical adjustments.
- **Day 3: Simulation & Final Insights** – The training concluded with a live classroom simulation to test the guides in practice, followed by a validation survey and a final presentation of results to academic stakeholders.

By the end of the workshop, the updated Toolkit was successfully validated, empowering participating universities to implement and scale these ethical AI frameworks across Latin America.
